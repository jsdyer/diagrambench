A local drawing board

Mermaid Tiny 12.3.0 is bundled with this page. Flowcharts, sequence diagrams, state diagrams, ER diagrams, timelines and Gantt charts render on your device. Mindmaps, architecture diagrams, KaTeX and ELK are outside this build.

Diagram code, imports and rendered output stay in this tab unless you deliberately use your Kroki server or download a file. Nothing is saved automatically. A project JSON keeps both the editable source and the rendered version; reopening it renders its source again.

A safe, static picture

Diagrams are rendered in an isolated frame, displayed as images and stripped of scripts, links and external resources. HTML labels and embedded configuration are disabled. SVG export is scalable; PNG exports at twice the diagram’s natural size, within a 4096-pixel limit.
